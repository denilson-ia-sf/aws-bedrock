TITLE: Corporate HR Vacation and Time-Off Policy
REVISION: 2026.2
SCOPE: All Full-Time Contract Employees

ELIGIBILITY:
Employees accrue vacation days monthly, starting from their first day. However, official vacation time can only be taken after completing 12 consecutive months of employment (1 full year).

REQUEST PROCESS:
1. All vacation requests must be submitted at least 30 calendar days in advance.
2. Requests must be submitted via the internal HR portal (Workday link).
3. Your immediate manager must approve the requested dates before your time off is finalized.

LIMITS & ROLLOVERS:
- Maximum consecutive vacation days: 15 business days (unless approved by HR).
- A maximum of 5 unused vacation days can be rolled over to the next calendar year. Any additional unused days will expire on December 31st.
